The Problem

Multi-practitioner nutrition practices struggle to manage patient records, anthropometric data, and skinfold measurements in a structured way. The result is time lost, data scattered across Excel files and paper folders, and diet plans that take hours instead of minutes to produce.

The Solution

NutriCare is the desktop software built for professional nutrition practices with multiple practitioners: it manages patients, visits, skinfold and anthropometric measurements, and generates personalised diet plans via AI in seconds — with PDF and Word export ready for the patient.

Why NutriCare

🤖 AI diet generation — the real differentiator

The nutritionist enters the patient's skinfold and anthropometric measurements and receives a complete, personalised diet plan from GPT-4o, Claude or Gemini in under 30 seconds. Review, export to PDF, move to the next patient. Same clinical quality, multiplied capacity.

  • Privacy by design: health data stays on the practice's server, no mandatory cloud
  • Bring your own API keys: no hidden AI subscription costs — choose any model
  • Multi-practitioner: separate accounts, independent patient histories per nutritionist
  • IT-friendly: MSI enterprise installer, silent install, Intune / SCCM deployable

Key Features

  • Patient records with full visit history, skinfold and anthropometric data
  • AI diet generation — 28+ models: GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini and more
  • PDF & Word export with practice header, ready to hand to the patient
  • Trend charts — weight, BMI, circumferences over time
  • Dashboard — aggregate stats and recent activity
  • Multi-practitioner — separate logins, independent patient histories per nutritionist
