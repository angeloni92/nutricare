The Problem

Nutritionists manage patients, visits, measurements, and dietary plans across disconnected tools — Excel spreadsheets, generic apps, paper folders — losing time, risking errors, and struggling to show patients their progress over time.

The Solution

NutriCare is the only desktop tool that covers the full professional workflow: from the first patient intake to AI-assisted diet plan generation, trend analysis of weight/BMI, and PDF/Word export ready to hand to the patient.

Why NutriCare

"Health data stays on the clinic's PC — not on third-party servers. GDPR compliance is structural, not a policy employees need to remember."
  • Privacy by design: no data sent to a proprietary cloud
  • No mandatory cloud AI subscription: users bring their own API keys
  • Multi-practitioner: multiple nutritionists on one PC, separate accounts
  • IT-friendly: MSI installer, silent install, Intune / SCCM deployable

Key Features

  • Patient management with complete visit history
  • AI diet generation — OpenAI GPT, Claude, Gemini
  • Professional export — PDF and Word with clinic header
  • Trend charts — weight, BMI, measurements over time
  • Studio dashboard — counters and recent activity
  • Multi-user login with role management
  • 30-day trial — machine-locked, offline activation
  • Updates via MSI reinstallation (silent upgrade-ready)
